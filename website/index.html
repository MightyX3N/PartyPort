How it works

  1. 1

    Join a party

    Start a party or paste an invite code from a friend. Everyone in the party can see who's online and what they're hosting.

  2. 2

    Someone hosts

    The host picks a game and PartyPort opens a playit.gg tunnel for it. Only the host needs a playit.gg account.

  3. 3

    It shows up in your game

    Click Join, open the game, and the server is listed under LAN, as if your friend were on your Wi-Fi.

Everything a LAN party needs

  • Party members only

    Servers only accept people in your party. Tick a box to let anyone with the address join too.

  • Party chat

    Talk to your party and get a ping with a Join button when someone starts a server.

  • Phone, tablet and PC

    The same app everywhere. Keeps running in the background while you play on your phone.

  • Works on playit.gg free

    Hosting works on a free playit.gg account. Premium unlocks extra tunnel types and custom domains.

  • Themes

    Modern by default, plus a classic VPN-client look and a navy terminal look. Or make your own.

  • Your IP stays private

    Traffic goes through playit.gg's relays, so nobody in the party sees your home IP address.

Questions

Do my friends need a playit.gg account?

No. Only the person hosting a game links a playit.gg account. Everyone else just installs PartyPort.

Can friends join without PartyPort?

Yes, if the host ticks "Allow joining without PartyPort". They then get a normal address to paste into the game.

What happens if the person who made the party is offline?

Nothing changes. Parties don't live on anyone's PC, so members stay in the party and invites keep working while the creator is away.

Does it work with Minecraft Bedrock on a phone?

Yes. Install PartyPort on the phone, join the party, and the server appears under Friends → LAN Games.

What about games without a LAN list?

For games like Terraria or Palworld, PartyPort gives you a local address such as 127.0.0.1:7777 to connect to.

Is this made by playit.gg?

No. PartyPort is an independent app that uses playit.gg's tunnels for hosting.
